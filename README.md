## The sentence

> Think of the post office, the library, and the market of every town on earth joined by wires, so a letter crosses the world in a breath and a reader may open the books of any library.

One sentence, 183 characters. It explains the modern internet to someone
arriving from 1800 using only things that person already knows — a post
office, a library, a market, a wire — with no word coined after their time
and no list of features: just one picture they could hold in their mind.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes the sentence to
`/output/entry.txt`. The output is deterministic and byte-identical on
every run.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
